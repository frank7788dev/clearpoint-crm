Download for Mac Download for Windows
  1. Mac: open the .dmg and drag ClearPoint CRM into Applications. The first time, if macOS says it can't be opened, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  2. Windows: run the installer. If Windows shows “protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Open the app and choose Create your login with the email Frank or your manager invited.
