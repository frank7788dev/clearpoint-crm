On your phone

Point your phone's camera at this code, or open frank7788dev.github.io/clearpoint-crm in your phone's browser.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open ClearPoint from your home screen and sign in.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open ClearPoint from your home screen and sign in.

On your computer

Download for MacDownload for Windows
  1. Mac: open the .dmg and drag ClearPoint CRM into Applications. If macOS says it can't be opened, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  2. Windows: run the installer. If Windows shows “protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Open the app and sign in, or choose Create your login with the email Frank or your manager invited.
